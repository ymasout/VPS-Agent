# Community Conduct

Project participation must remain professional, technically honest, and safe.

- Treat contributors and users with respect regardless of background or experience.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Do not publish another person's private infrastructure, credentials, logs, security findings, or personal information.
- Do not pressure maintainers to bypass reviews, safety checks, license checks, or responsible disclosure.
- Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, impersonation, and deliberate disruption are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and community. Security-sensitive conduct should follow `SECURITY.md`; ordinary conduct concerns should be raised privately with the repository owner through an agreed contact channel. A permanent private conduct-reporting channel must be documented before a broader community launch.
